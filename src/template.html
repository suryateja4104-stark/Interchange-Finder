      About this data

      Seat availability and live running status are not included in this version. The map joins station coordinates stop by stop, so lines are approximate and not the exact track; the faint grey lines are every train's printed stops. State boundaries come from the open india-maps-data set by udit-001 (district boundaries, merged into states and simplified), so they are indicative only. Times are the printed timetable times, so a train that has been retimed since will differ. Each leg is a separate ticket, so a missed connection is not covered; keep waits of 90 minutes or more where you can. Check IRCTC or NTES before you book. Stations that the timetable does not print cannot be searched; if your destination is missing, pick the nearest big station and add a bus or taxi leg.
